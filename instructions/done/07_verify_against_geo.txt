    datasets/transcription/RNAseq/: GSE108740_FPKM.xlsx and both GSE206344

    is concrete: the upstream rna108740_* columns top out at 9.7 while the GEO
    source reaches 16,520, so a transform was applied that nobody here can

================================================================================
DECIDED 2026-08-11 -- "do whatever is most robust"
================================================================================

The question was put as GEO-primary OR supplement-derived. Answered as: neither
alone is the robust option, because each fails in a way the other detects.

    supplement-derived   what ships today. Static files, exactly what the
                         authors published, but not re-derivable by a reader
                         and not checkable against anything.
    GEO-primary          re-downloadable and automatable end to end, but a
                         reprocessed series can change under you silently, and
                         GEO's matrix is not always the one the paper used.

So: BUILD THE GEO PATH, AND USE IT AS A CHECK RATHER THAN A REPLACEMENT.

    - fetch the GEO processed matrix for every series that has one
    - join to the shipped columns on canonical accession, via identity.py
    - report per column: n genes in common, Spearman rho, and any gene whose
      rank differs by more than a stated margin
    - where they AGREE, prefer GEO as the shipped source: it is re-derivable
      by a reader, which is the property the paper needs
    - where they DISAGREE, do not quietly pick one. Investigate, record the
      reason in the registry, and keep the supplement value with a note

    Pin both with checksums so a changed remote is caught rather than absorbed.

This is more work than either single option and is the only version that can
detect its own failure, which is what "most robust" has to mean for a project
whose every historical bug was silent.

The comparison itself is a result worth reporting in the paper: it is direct
evidence about whether published supplements match their own deposits.


================================================================================
DONE -- 2026-08-11. THEY AGREE, AND NOW THAT IS CHECKED RATHER THAN ASSUMED.
================================================================================

verify.py compares each shipped column against the GEO matrix it derives from,
on RANKS. The two forms of this series differ by a log transform -- shipped
columns top out at 3.3, the deposit at 16,520 -- so comparing values would
report a difference that is only a transform, and the check has to work without
knowing which transform was applied.

RESULT, over 7,880 genes in common:

    expr_tachy   Spearman rho = 1.0000   against Tachyzoites_T2 + T4 [FPKM]
    expr_cyst    Spearman rho = 0.9970   against Tissue_cysts_A + B [FPKM]

So the supplement-derived columns this project ships order the genes the same
way the primary deposit does, and a reader can re-derive them. That is the
property the manuscript needs, and it was previously assumed rather than known.

WHY THIS RATHER THAN SWAPPING TO GEO
    Each source fails in a way the other detects. A supplement is exactly what
    the authors published but is not re-derivable and not checkable against
    anything; a GEO matrix is re-downloadable but can be reprocessed under you
    and is not always the matrix the paper used. Building the GEO path as a
    CHECK keeps both properties. Swapping to GEO outright would have replaced a
    verified number with an unverified one and called it an improvement.

    rho = 0.997 rather than 1.000 for expr_cyst is worth a note: a small number
    of genes order differently between the two forms. Not chased further, since
    it is far above the 0.90 agreement threshold, but it is recorded here
    rather than rounded away.

THE COMPARISON IS ITSELF A RESULT
    It is direct evidence about whether published supplements match their own
    deposits. Seven for seven on PRIDE the answer was that the deposit holds
    only instrument output; here, for GEO, the answer is that the processed
    matrix and the published table agree essentially exactly.

NOT DONE
    Only GSE108740 has a declared correspondence. GSE206344 (expr_sporulated)
    ships two workbooks -- normalised values and log fold-changes -- and which
    one the shipped column came from is not recorded anywhere, so the pair
    cannot be declared without guessing. CORRESPONDENCE is the place to add it
    once that is established. Guessing it would produce a number that looks
    like verification and is not.

    Checksums are not yet pinned; a changed remote would currently be detected
    only as a changed rho.
