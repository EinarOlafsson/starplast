WHAT THE RESULTS ACTUALLY WERE
    Block weights now control influence. hyperLOPIT had been taking 56% of the
    matrix by having 27 one-hot columns; robust scaling had inflated the
    published screens to half. Four blocks at weight 1.0 now give 23.5% each.

    F1 was overstated when first reported and the correction stands: median
    imputation was described as leaking study effort into position. Measured,
    the gap survives dropping the offending column and missingness between
    assays correlates at only r = 0.07-0.15, so there is no single "was this
    gene studied" factor. Much of the gap is real biology that missingness
    tracks. The defect was that the choice was hidden, not that the gap exists.
    drop_genes was also a silent no-op and now works -- revealing that only 3
    of 8,140 genes have complete data.

    V1 changed the LOPIT design. orthoLOPIT is 71.8% accurate overall (4.0x
    chance) but that average hides everything: two donors agreeing gives 96.3%,
    Plasmodium alone 65.9%; proteasome 100%, ER 97.5%, but cytosol 35.7% and
    golgi/secretory 6.1%. Transfers are gated on their own validated accuracy.
    Accepted ones are 93.2% correct, at the cost of 126 -> 28 labels.

    P1 did not produce edges, deliberately. Median genes per parsed study is
    754 and the largest lists 7,866 -- these are complete quantification
    tables. Treating membership as interaction would have invented ~50,000
    false edges.
