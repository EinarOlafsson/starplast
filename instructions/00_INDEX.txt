================================================================================
WHAT STARPLAST IS
================================================================================

A PyQt6 desktop application. 8,140 *Toxoplasma gondii* genes are points in a
3D space; the relations between them are toggleable edges; clicking a gene
shows everything known about it, including when the answer is "nothing".

Terminal entry point: `starplast`

It is the front end for the information map -- entities and relations pulled
out of the literature, with inference over the map's *structural holes* rather
than over what authors said their gaps were.

The single idea the whole thing is built around: **most of this proteome has
never been studied, and any tool that does not say so will mislead you.** 5,574
of 8,140 genes are named in no paper at all. 71% of the genes that ARE named
appear only in a screen's hit table, never in a title or abstract. So every
layer distinguishes measurement from inference from absence, and refuses to
let one read as another.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE DATA MODEL — TWO SHAPES, AND PEOPLE CONFUSE THEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    data/nodes.parquet       one row per GENE       8,140 x 165 columns
    data/graph.npz           one row per GENE PAIR  12 edge types, ~296k edges
    data/mentions.parquet    gene x document        which paper named which gene
    data/crosslink_models.parquet   one crosslinked pair, its residues + models

A crosslink is inherently pairwise -- "GRA7 residue 124 joins GRA2 residue 102"
-- so it cannot be a property of one gene. The node table carries only counts
(`n_xlink_partners`); the pairs live in graph.npz. Same for co-mention,
structural similarity and IP-MS. Asking "is the crosslink data in the table"
gets the answer "only as a summary".

The cache is 11 MB, committed, and self-contained: the application needs no
network, no dataset and no build step. Only 3D coordinates for structures are
fetched on demand.

THE MODULES, IN THE ORDER DATA FLOWS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    datasets.py       the provenance registry -- 26 datasets, machine-readable.
                      datasets.unresolved() lists citations still unconfirmed.
    identity.py       every string the literature uses for a gene -> one
                      canonical accession, tagged by confidence
    corpus.py         abstracts + PMC JATS -> one sectioned Document stream
    literature.py     -> mentions.parquet, the auditable intermediate
    localisation.py   hyperLOPIT MAP + MCMC, and orthoLOPIT transfer
    screens.py        four published CRISPR screens + iBAQ
    expression.py     six further expression datasets (in vivo, stress, MORC,
                      proteome, phospho, oocyst)
    sources.py        download from GEO/PRIDE; normalise by quantification type
    interactions.py   crosslink MS, IP-MS, Foldseek structural similarity
    interaction_studies.py  97 tagged-protein studies -> membership
    structures.py     coordinates on demand (local mirror, else AlphaFold DB)
    build_graph.py    composes all of it into the cache
    ---
    embedding.py      configurable matrix: blocks, NaN policy, scaling, weights
    clustering.py     DBSCAN/HDBSCAN walks + the cluster hypothesis battery
    tuning.py         seeded UMAP walk, embedding store, CSV import
    search.py         THE POINT: walk dataset combos, score held-out recovery
    ---
    theme.py          4 palettes, colour maps by kind, point styles
    app.py            the browser
    analysis_panel.py the analysis tab (data -> map -> clusters -> meaning ->
                      search)


================================================================================
WHAT A NEXT SESSION MUST NOT REDISCOVER
================================================================================

Each of these cost real time to find. Most were silent failures: the code ran,
the output looked fine, the numbers were wrong.

IDENTITY
  Papers cite whatever accession was current when they were written. One 2019
  in vivo screen uses pre-2012 `TGME49_0xxxxx` ids for EVERY gene and
  contributed 0 of 8,140 rows until routed through identity.py; then 168.
  `TGGT1_` strain accessions are MORE common than `TGME49_` in interaction
  supplements. Any new dataset join has this failure mode.

  StarPath is the exception to the suffix rule: it uses RH88 accessions whose
  numbering does NOT correspond to ME49. TGRH88_016370 is TGME49_210408, not
  _216370. Use the alias column the export ships.

SYMBOL MATCHING
  HOOK, CLAMP, CLIP, SPARK and REMIND are all real Toxoplasma gene symbols and
  all ordinary English words. Digit-free symbols must appear in UPPER CASE or
  the corpus fills with prose. Tokenise Unicode-aware: an ASCII class truncates
  French "Sante" to "Sant", which is also a real symbol.

  "GT1" is both a strain name and the symbol of TGME49_214320 (glucose
  transporter). Strain designations are blocked outright.

CIRCULARITY
  This is the one that matters. Naming the columns an embedding used is NOT
  enough to keep a finding honest. `compartment` fed one embedding and the
  battery duly reported its exact twin `lopit_map` and its derivations
  `lopit_unified`, `compartment_best` as the top held-out discoveries at
  V = 0.96. Association with the inputs is now MEASURED; anything >= 0.95 is
  marked derived, and every result carries `assoc_with_input`.

  The size of the effect: with hyperLOPIT leaked in, "LOPIT separates the
  clusters at V = 0.96". Held out properly, the best of 164 runs reaches
  mean F1 0.362. The guard is the difference between a spectacular artefact
  and a modest truth.

CONFOUNDS IN THE DERIVED LAYERS
  Structural holes: orthogroup and domain are ONE evidence family, not two --
  paralogs almost always share domains. Counted separately, 53 of the first 66
  candidates were pure paralogy. Letting homology pair with expression admitted
  291 more, 76% same-orthogroup. Both phenotype measurements are required.
  compartment is excluded entirely: too unspecific, and it tracks abundance.

QUANTIFICATION
  The same GEO series demonstrates both ways to get this wrong, and neither is
  visible in the output. GEO's GSE108740_FPKM.xlsx reaches 16,520 and is real
  FPKM -- skip the log and one gene dominates every distance. The columns
  derived from it in the node table, still named rna108740_*_FPKM, top out at
  9.7 because they were logged upstream -- log them again and real variation
  compresses to nothing. THE RANGE DECIDES, NOT THE FILENAME.

  The 8 CRISPR screens share neither sign convention nor scale. Naive-BMDM and
  IFN-gamma are inverted; SDs differ ~15x. The two GRA12 screens are NOT
  replicates (in-vivo L2FC r = 0.41). Rank-normalise before pooling anything.

WHAT SUPPLEMENTS ACTUALLY CONTAIN
  A supplement is usually the paper's COMPLETE quantification table, not its
  hit list. Median genes per parsed interaction study is 754; the largest lists
  7,866 -- essentially the whole proteome. Treating membership as interaction
  would manufacture ~50,000 false edges. That is why
  interaction_studies.py reports membership and stops there.

  PRIDE deposits RAW instrument files. Checked across PXD019729, PXD065585 and
  PXD043808: not one RESULT file. Per-protein quantification exists only in the
  papers' supplementary tables. GEO, by contrast, deposits processed matrices
  and can be automated end to end.

RENDERING
  GLScatterPlotItem blends ADDITIVELY by default. 8,140 overlapping points
  summed to white and every colour mode rendered as one featureless blob --
  while every array-level test passed, because they checked the colour array
  and never the render. RENDER THE APP AND LOOK AT IT before believing a
  display claim.

  pyqtgraph ships no "coolwarm": its diverging maps come from the CET set.
  Offering a matplotlib name raises FileNotFoundError inside a paint call.

  pyqtgraph's `projectionMatrix()` signature has changed twice: no arguments,
  then (region=None), now (region, viewport). app.py tries all three.

ENVIRONMENT
  pytest-qt imports PySide6 at plugin load, so pyqtgraph binds to PySide6 while
  the app hard-imports PyQt6 -- the app then fails to import under pytest while
  working perfectly when launched. PYQTGRAPH_QT_LIB=PyQt6 is pinned in app.py
  and tests/conftest.py.

  The working copy sits in the Syncthing tree, so .git replicates to the work
  machine. Commit from ONE machine at a time or Syncthing forks objects inside
  .git.
