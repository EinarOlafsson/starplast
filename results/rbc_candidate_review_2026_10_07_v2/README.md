The executed notebook checks the publisher Table S3 headers, all four donors in
two fractions, every copy-count value, mapping ambiguity/reverse collisions,
donor rank correlations and all six installed RBC measurement columns.
Inputs and outputs are pinned by `manifest.json`. Numerical source/derivative
tables remain external; only audit counts, summaries and associations are here.

2,653 published groups: 2,195 have one reviewed candidate, 330 have multiple and
128 have none. Of 2,120 target proteins, 59 receive more than one group. With
both ambiguity and reverse collisions withheld, 2,061 single-protein projections
remain. Original groups and eight donor copy-count values are preserved.

Decision: keep installed fraction PSMs and selective surface-profiling evidence;
the candidate answers a complementary absolute-abundance question. A surface
proteome cannot be replaced with a whole-cell/white-ghost abundance table simply
because both report copy estimates. Candidate admission under 65.04 still needs
a common assay comprehensiveness denominator, rights-compatible pack and
source-specific projection/benchmark validation. The source's CC BY-NC metadata
is explicit; no original quantitative table enters the wheel.

268 citations yield 29.115/year as of its actual retrieval date. The separate
preference-factor notebook in the prior review records recency factor 1.01587,
unknown comprehensiveness and no admission-gated score. No source is certified
literature-best. 548 host/source checks and 625 affected review/registry/selection/
catalogue checks passed. Initial incomplete surface comparison is retained and
superseded; no installed values were changed in either review.
