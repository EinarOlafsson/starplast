Weighted F1

For each label, F1 of its hidden genes against the ONE cluster chosen for it on visible genes; averaged with labels weighted by their hidden genes. A label with no cluster scores 0.

Range: 0 to 1

Chance: set by permuting hidden labels over the same clusters (the verdict's null)

Whether a label that nobody showed the clustering falls out as a cluster, scored on genes the choice never saw.

Weighted precision

For each label, the share of hidden genes in its chosen cluster that carry it; label-size weighted.

Range: 0 to 1

Chance: about the label's share

How pure the chosen clusters are.

Weighted recall

For each label, the share of its hidden genes that landed in its chosen cluster; label-size weighted.

Range: 0 to 1

Chance: about the cluster's share of the genes

How completely each label is gathered into one cluster.

Adjusted Rand index (ARI)

Agreement between the clustering and the hidden labels over all pairs of hidden genes, corrected so random partitions of the same sizes score 0.

Range: -0.5 to 1

Chance: 0

Whole-partition agreement, independent of which cluster was chosen for which label.

Normalised mutual information (NMI)

Mutual information between clusters and hidden labels, divided by the mean of their entropies.

Range: 0 to 1

Chance: small but above 0 for many small clusters

How much knowing a gene's cluster tells you about its label.

Homogeneity

1 minus the uncertainty about the label left once the cluster is known, relative to the label's own entropy.

Range: 0 to 1

Chance: near 0

Whether each cluster holds one label.

Completeness

1 minus the uncertainty about the cluster left once the label is known, relative to the clustering's entropy.

Range: 0 to 1

Chance: near 0

Whether each label sits in one cluster. Its harmonic mean with homogeneity is the V-measure, which equals NMI.

Unclustered share

Share of hidden genes HDBSCAN left as noise (in no cluster).

Range: 0 to 1

Chance: not applicable

Context: noise genes cannot be recovered by any cluster.
