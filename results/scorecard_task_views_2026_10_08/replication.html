Whether the findings are properties of the genes or of the sample they were found in.

Findings made

Range: 0 upward

Replication by chance

The replication rate with the second half's evidence scrambled, averaged over runs.

Range: 0 to 1

Chance: is itself the chance level

What replication looks like for findings with nothing behind them.

Replication lift

Replication rate divided by the replication rate by chance.

Range: 0 upward

Chance: 1

How many times more often real findings replicate than chance ones.
